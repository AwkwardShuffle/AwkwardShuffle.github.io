Campaigns · UI / UX · Email · Brand

Kenny Carmack is a visual designer focused on clear systems across digital, print, and product experiences.

I work across campaign systems, UI/UX, email, direct mail, and brand design, with an emphasis on strong hierarchy, repeatable visual systems, and communication that is easy to understand and use.

01 / Campaign Systems

Cross-channel visual communication

Professional campaign work for Nature’s Sunshine built to stay coherent as messaging moves across email, direct mail, and related campaign touchpoints.

01

Made for More

A four-regimen Nature’s Sunshine campaign carried across coordinated email and a trifold direct-mail piece. Each regimen uses its own visual personality while staying connected through a shared campaign structure, typography, product presentation, and messaging system.

Campaign System Email Design Direct Mail Cross-channel
The system needed to support four distinct wellness stories—More Active, More Nourished, More Present, and More Refreshed—without fragmenting the broader campaign identity. The result is a family of pieces that can flex by regimen while remaining visibly connected.

Trifold direct mail

The print piece brings all four regimens together in one compact overview, using the same color-coded campaign system to make the full offer easy to scan.

Four regimens, one email system

Each email adapts the same campaign language to a different regimen while preserving a recognizable family structure across the full set.

04 / Brand & Editorial

Brand & editorial work

Selected identity, motion, and editorial projects exploring typography, image-making, systems, and visual storytelling.

01

RAT:O / Ratio

A conceptual identity system for Ratio, a fictional design studio. I began with a custom wordmark, then expanded the geometry of the mark into a flexible circuit-board-inspired motif whose dots echo the punctuation and forms within the logo. The system was applied across a branded stationery set to create a cohesive visual language.

Brand Identity Stationery Motion Design After Effects
The logo animation extends the same idea into motion. Built in After Effects, it plays with shifting proportion and scale to reference the “ratio” concept while keeping the movement playful and visually engaging.
02

BEAT Magazine

An editorial concept for BEAT, a fictional magazine for drummers, centered on Art Blakey. The visual direction draws from classic Blue Note album covers: bold type, high-contrast photography, and a restrained graphic system. I created a double-exposure portrait in Photoshop and used circular forms throughout the spreads to echo drums and give the layout a consistent visual rhythm.

Editorial Design Typography Photoshop Layout
The project uses repetition and scale to connect the photography, typography, and circular drum motif, creating an editorial system that feels energetic without losing readability.
